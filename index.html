Eswatini

Eswatini,[b] formally the Kingdom of Eswatini (historically called KaNgwane[12]), also known by its former official names Swaziland[c] and the Kingdom of Swaziland,[13][14] is a landlocked country in Southern Africa. It is bordered by South Africa on all sides except the east, where it shares a border with Mozambique. its climate and topography are diverse, ranging from a cool and mountainous highveld to a hot and dry lowveld. The executive capital is Mbabane, and the legislative capital is Lobamba.
